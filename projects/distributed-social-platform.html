THE PROBLEM / THE WORK

Why this exists

Stateful services that handle messages, feeds, and user interactions require carefully managed concurrency. I explored an actor-based design with supervised processes to separate responsibilities and reason about resilience.

How I approached it

The project implements social-platform features in Gleam and Erlang/OTP, including threaded conversations, messaging, voting, and feeds. It explores fault-tolerant process organization using OTP supervision trees and ETS, with concurrent-load benchmarking.

Under the hood

  • Modeled user-facing functions as isolated, communicating process responsibilities.
  • Used OTP supervision concepts to explore recovery and fault containment.
  • Used ETS as an in-memory data facility within the Erlang ecosystem.
  • Benchmarked simulated concurrent workloads of up to 1,000 users.
Research and evaluation integrity: The benchmark describes simulated load, not production user traffic. The architecture illustration is conceptual and is not a literal diagram extracted from the codebase.
